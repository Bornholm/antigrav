Soundwave is a racing game where every track is built from a piece of music. Pick a song, one of yours or one that ships with the game, and the track comes out of it: it speeds up when the song takes off, climbs before a drop and dives on it, narrows and twists when the beat hits hard. The scenery pulses with the frequencies and the track changes colour with the intensity.

A race lasts as long as the song. You fly at over 1,000 km/h, pick up bonuses to attack or protect yourself, and race against up to seven opponents.

• Nine built-in songs in three cups: Neon, Steel and Abyss, each with its own scenery
• Your own MP3, Ogg Vorbis, FLAC or WAV files become tracks
• Three difficulty levels, a score and a grade from D to S at every finish
• Local Wi-Fi multiplayer, single races or cups, the host sends the song to the other players
• Touch or gamepad controls
• Three graphics quality levels for modest phones

Soundwave shows no ads, sells nothing and collects no data. The game works without an Internet connection.

It is free software, released under the GNU GPL version 3. The source code is at https://github.com/Bornholm/soundwave

Requires Android 13 or later and a phone that supports Vulkan 1.1.
